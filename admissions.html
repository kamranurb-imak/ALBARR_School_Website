How to Apply

Simple 4-Step Process

Applying to AL-BARR SCHOOLING SYSTEM is straightforward. Follow these steps to secure your child's place.

1

Submit Enquiry

Fill in our online enquiry form or call us directly. Our admissions team will contact you within 1 business day.

2

Submit Documents

Bring the required documents to our school office or upload them digitally via email to admissions@albarr.edu.pk.

3

Entrance Assessment

Students applying to Class IV and above sit a short assessment in English and Mathematics to help us place them appropriately.

4

Confirm Enrolment

Upon acceptance, confirm your child's place by paying the registration fee and completing the enrolment forms.

Requirements

Documents Required

📋Completed application / enquiry form
📸2 recent passport-size photographs
🪪Copy of student's B-Form / CNIC
🪪Copy of parent / guardian CNIC
📜Previous school leaving / transfer certificate
📊Last two years' academic result cards
🏅Cambridge result slip (for O-Level/A-Level applicants)
